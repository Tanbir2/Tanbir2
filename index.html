My Journey

My journey in electrical engineering began with a strong academic foundation during my undergraduate studies, where I developed core knowledge in power systems, circuit analysis, and electrical machines. During this time, I became driven to understand how modern power systems can be made more reliable, efficient, and intelligent.

As part of my undergraduate work, I completed a research-focused thesis on transmission line fault classification using machine learning. By applying MLPNN and ensemble learning algorithms to improve fault detection accuracy, I gained early experience in integrating data-driven methods with traditional power engineering.

To further advance my expertise, I am currently completing my Master of Engineering (MEng) in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Calgary, specializing in power systems and sustainable energy. This program has deepened my understanding of industrial power systems, renewable energy integration, and the application of machine learning in large-scale engineering.

Alongside my academic studies, I have built practical, industry-relevant skills using PSS®E, PSCAD™, ETAP, MATLAB/Simulink, Python, and AutoCAD. I have applied these tools in projects involving load flow analysis, fault studies, and data-driven power system modeling. One of my key achievements is developing an automated 8760-hour power system reliability and curtailment analysis framework using Python and PSS®E.

My goal is to build a diverse and dynamic engineering career—spanning transmission, protection, system operations, design, and project coordination—while contributing to the development of reliable and sustainable energy infrastructure through intelligent technologies and automation.
